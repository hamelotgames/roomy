Privacy Policy

Roomy • Hamelot Games

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Privacy at a glance: Roomy's standard features do not require a Google account. Optional YouTube integration uses Google OAuth to connect an account and process livestream events and chat commands. This policy explains the data used, why it is used, the measures protecting it, and how users can disconnect or revoke access.

1. Introduction

Hamelot Games ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Roomy accesses, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when people use Roomy, including its optional Google, YouTube, and Twitch integrations.

Roomy is a desktop application featuring virtual environments, character customization, interactive activities, and optional livestream integration. Connecting a Google account is optional and is not required for Roomy's standard features.

2. Information We Access

When you choose to connect YouTube, Roomy may access information allowed by the Google permissions you approve, including:

  • Your YouTube channel identifier, channel name, and publicly available channel details.
  • Information about active livestreams associated with your account.
  • Livestream chat messages, public display names, and channel identifiers necessary to recognize chat commands and participation.
  • OAuth authorization tokens necessary to access the approved YouTube features.

Roomy uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization process. You sign in with Google directly; Roomy does not request or store your Google password.

3. How Information Is Used

Roomy uses information obtained through Google services to provide user-enabled features, such as:

  • Connecting your YouTube account: identifying the authorized channel and establishing a connection.
  • Reading livestream chat: receiving messages to recognize supported commands and events.
  • Interactive activities: allowing viewers to join and interact with supported activities, including Battle Royale.
  • Connection management: maintaining the authorized session as needed for enabled features.

We do not use Google user data for targeted advertising, marketing profiles, credit decisions, or training general-purpose AI models. We do not sell Google user data.

4. Storage and Processing

Roomy's YouTube integration is designed to process authorized YouTube information on the user's computer. OAuth session information may be stored in Roomy's local application data so users can reconnect without authorizing every session.

Livestream messages are processed to perform enabled interactions. Activity-related information, such as a viewer's display name and participation state, may be held temporarily while an activity is running.

Hamelot Games does not operate a central service intended to collect Roomy users' Google OAuth tokens or archive their YouTube livestream chats. Standard Roomy integration is not intended to create permanent chat archives.

5. Data Security and Protection

Google OAuth Authentication

Roomy authenticates through Google's OAuth 2.0 process. Users grant permissions through Google, and Roomy does not see or store their Google account passwords. OAuth tokens are used to make API requests within the authorization granted by the user.

Encrypted Data Transmission

Connections to Google's authentication and API endpoints use HTTPS with Transport Layer Security (TLS). TLS protects data transmitted between the application and Google against interception and unauthorized modification while in transit.

Local Storage and Device Security

Roomy is designed to keep saved authentication session information in its application-specific local user-data directory rather than sending it to a Hamelot Games authentication server. Protection of locally stored information also depends on the user's operating system, device account permissions, and device security.

Important distinction: HTTPS encrypts data in transit; it does not, by itself, encrypt local files stored on a computer. We do not claim that locally saved OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest unless such protection is implemented and verified.

Limited Access and Purpose

Roomy uses authorized Google API information only for supported features. The application's requested permissions should be limited to those necessary to provide its YouTube functionality. Authentication tokens are not intentionally exposed to livestream viewers.

Account Control

Users can stop using the YouTube integration and revoke Google's authorization at any time. For removal of any saved local session data, see the data deletion section below.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

Hamelot Games does not sell, rent, or trade Google user data to advertisers or data brokers. Roomy communicates with Google's services as needed to authenticate users and provide authorized functionality.

Public YouTube display names and activity participation information may be shown within Roomy as part of interactive activities and may be visible to the person broadcasting Roomy or to their livestream audience. Private OAuth tokens and Google account passwords are not displayed as part of these activities.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

OAuth sessions: Local authentication information may be retained on the user's computer so they can reconnect. Users can stop using the integration and remove local authentication information from the application's data directory.

Chat data: Livestream chat messages are processed for active features and are not intended to be permanently archived by Roomy's standard YouTube integration.

Activity information: Names and participation details may be kept in memory for the active activity and are generally discarded when the activity ends or the application closes.

Revoking access: Users can revoke Roomy's Google account authorization through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized access through the revoked grant. To delete any locally saved copies of authentication data, users should also clear the applicable Roomy application data.

Privacy requests: To ask questions about deletion or information handling, contact hamelotgames@gmail.com. Because the standard integration operates locally, Hamelot Games may not have a copy of information saved on individual users' computers.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

Roomy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is intended to follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

  • Google information is used to deliver or improve user-requested features.
  • Google user data is not sold to third parties or used for targeted advertising.
  • Google user data is not used to determine creditworthiness.
  • Google user data is not used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Use of Google information is limited to the purposes described in this policy.

9. Third-Party Services

Roomy may offer optional integrations with Google and YouTube for account authorization, livestream information, and chat-based interactions, and Twitch for comparable livestream functionality. Each service has its own privacy policy and terms.

See Google's Privacy Policy, YouTube's Terms of Service, and Twitch's Privacy Notice.

10. Your Choices

  • Choose whether to connect a YouTube account.
  • Decline or revoke Google authorization.
  • Disable optional livestream features.
  • Remove saved local authentication information.
  • Contact Hamelot Games with privacy concerns or requests.

Declining YouTube integration does not stop you from using Roomy's standard features that do not require YouTube access.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy to reflect changes in application features, privacy practices, or applicable requirements. The effective date at the top shows the latest published revision. Material changes will be disclosed through an updated policy and, when appropriate, additional notices.
